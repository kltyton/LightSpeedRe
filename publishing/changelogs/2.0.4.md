# LightSpeedRe 2.0.4 (Forge 1.20.1)

- Initialize NetMusic's renderer-owned models when its music player and big megaphone items are rendered, preserving lazy renderer creation.
- Adapt registry holder hooks to newer Forge 47.4 releases while retaining the legacy dispatch path.
- Write client asset snapshots on the cache executor and preserve updates made while a snapshot is being written.
- Keep configured resource caches and parallel loading enabled when Sinytra Connector is installed.

## 中文

- 在渲染 NetMusic 音乐机和大喇叭物品时初始化其所需模型，保留渲染器延迟创建。
- 适配新版 Forge 47.4 的注册表引用分发，保留旧版分发路径。
- 客户端资源快照改由缓存线程写入，并保留写入期间发生的更新。
- 共装 Sinytra Connector 时保留配置中的资源缓存和并行加载设置。
